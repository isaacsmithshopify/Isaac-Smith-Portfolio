How we'll work together

A simple process, no surprises.

  1. Discovery

    We talk about your goals, your customers and what's not working today. You get a clear scope and quote before anything starts.

  2. Design

    I design the look and structure of your key pages. You review it and request changes before any code is written.

  3. Build

    I build it, tested on phones, tablets and desktops. You see progress along the way rather than waiting for a big reveal.

  4. Launch and support

    I handle the launch, check everything works, and show you how to manage your site. I'm available if you need help afterwards.

Questions

Before you get in touch.

Can you work on my existing Shopify store?

Yes. I can customize your current theme, add features, fix layout problems and improve the shopping experience without rebuilding from scratch.

Do I need to have my content ready?

Not completely. It helps to have your logo, photos and a rough idea of your pages, and I can guide you on the rest during discovery.

Will my site work on mobile?

Yes. Every site I build is designed mobile-first and tested on phones, tablets and desktops before launch.

Will I be able to update the site myself?

Yes. I'll show you how to edit content, products and images, and I'm available if you need help.
